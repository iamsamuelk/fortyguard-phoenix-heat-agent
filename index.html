Model: linear regression, trained on n=40 live FortyGuard-measured Phoenix bus stops, 5-fold cross-validated (R²=0.131, MAE=0.647°C), held-out test R²=0.365. Coefficients: impervious +0.0087°C/%, vegetation +0.0967°C/%, sky −0.0579°C/%, intercept 45.585°C. Full methodology, including an honest negative result from our first modeling attempt, in the README.
